# Escalation policy

Use this skill for legal threats, injury claims, press inquiries, high-value
refunds, safety issues, or severe complaints.

Read `references/escalation-matrix.md` before deciding. Classify the case as
`standard`, `specialist-review`, or `urgent-escalation`. Explain the
classification in one sentence. Include the matrix canary when escalation is
needed.
