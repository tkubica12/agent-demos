# NNNN. Title in sentence case

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** roles or names

## Context

What situation forces a decision now. Include the constraints that are real and
verifiable: platform behaviour, timings, quotas, permissions, audience, delivery risk.
Record evidence rather than assumption, with dates or versions when behaviour is
time-sensitive.

## Decision drivers

- driver, stated so that a reader can tell whether a future option satisfies it
- driver

## Options considered

### Option A - name

What it is, and what it would cost in effort, risk and delivery reliability.

### Option B - name

What it is, and why it was not chosen.

## Decision

The option chosen, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what work this creates. Include the
operational consequences: setup time, failure modes, cleanup, permissions, cost.

## Assumptions and revisit triggers

State the assumptions this decision rests on, and the specific conditions that would
make it invalid. A decision without revisit triggers cannot be reviewed honestly.

- assumption, and the observation that would disprove it
- condition that should reopen this decision

## Validation

How the decision was verified in practice: the exact checks run, what was observed,
and any evidence identifiers such as request IDs or test runs.
